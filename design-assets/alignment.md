## Design fixtures versus game implementation

All required new moments are designed and browsable. Review eligibility, Reputation thresholds, odds, exact effect values, evidence targets and recap totals are fixtures. Numeric balancing and a complete deterministic career engine are not implemented by this design package. The keyed narrative data, yearly salary and fictional names are not claims about real companies or people.

Promotion/stay/firing, job acceptance, second-firing routing, nine-slot promotion, new-squad save recovery, postmortem cost/refund and keynote routing were exercised in browser checks. All 35 selectable states pass layout checks in both themes at 320px and 390px. See validation.json for results.
