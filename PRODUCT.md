# EM27 product context

## Register
product

## Users
Engineers and engineering managers playing in one-handed, 3–5 minute sessions on phones, in daylight or indoors. Twelve weeks form a quarter. Local saves allow stopping mid-week.

## Product Purpose
A strategy game about running a six-person engineering team. Eight calendar slots force tradeoffs between people, delivery and politics. Friday tests the plan; every changed number has a visible cause. Source of approved direction: designer-brief.html and DESIGN.md.

## Brand Personality
Satirical, grounded, numerate. Championship Manager 01/02 meets a 2000s corporate intranet. Familiar industry situations and fictional people, with insider vocabulary.

## Anti-references
No real company logos or imitations of their visual identities. No real people. No spacious marketing dashboard, decorative illustrations, sign-in flow, free-text replies or sound. No hidden mechanical surprises from generated prose.

## Design Principles
- Put the time budget within sight throughout planning.
- Explain the cost and likely effects before commitment.
- Use dense summary rows and readable drill-downs on phones.
- Make ignored demands explicit choices with consequences.
- Keep people and promises memorable across weeks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
The brief requires 44px minimum touch targets, no hover-only interactions, variable-length text, and both light and dark themes. Use text and symbols alongside semantic color, keyboard focus indicators, and reduced-motion support as implementation accommodations.

## Confirmed visual reference, revision 2

The user rejected the initial modern intranet treatment and supplied a Championship Manager 01/02 screenshot. Follow its actual visual vocabulary: blue navigation, purple tabs, red title bars, compact grey translucent stat rows, yellow-green figures, restrained bevels, and scene imagery behind the data. Keep mobile touch targets, persistent calendar and original game identity. The office backdrop is original SVG artwork, not copied football imagery.

## Updated brief alignment, revision 3

A run spans a career: EM → Senior EM → Director (win). EM has 6 engineers and 8 slots; Senior EM has 8 engineers and 9 slots. Two consecutive Exceeds-or-better quarters plus sufficient Reputation enable promotion, with a packet also required at Google. Does Not Meet or two consecutive Partially Meets triggers firing. First firing opens a 1–3 offer job hunt at another company, at the same or one lower level; second firing ends the run. Strong quarters can generate poaching offers with accept/decline/leverage choices. End every run with a shareable portrait recap including history and squad hall of fame. Reputation persists across jobs. Vercel has a fixed keynote, Stripe has next-week incident reviews, Google has promotion evidence and calibration. These are locked MVP scope from the updated brief, not speculative future work.
